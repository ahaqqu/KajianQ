# LLM-translated kitab at ingestion, labeled as machine translation

Most pre-600 H kitab have no Indonesian translation, but KajianQ's audience does not read Arabic and the embedding strategy concatenates Arabic + Indonesian text. We LLM-translate every kitab chunk at ingestion into `text_indonesia`, always displayed with the label "Terjemahan mesin — lihat teks Arab asli" alongside the Arabic original. Chosen over (a) Indonesian summaries only — paraphrases defeat strict citation — and (b) ingesting only already-translated kitab — which would gut the pre-600 H moat since almost none have Indonesian translations. Mistranslation risk is accepted and mitigated by the visible label, the always-present Arabic, and faithfulness spot-checks in eval. `text_raw` is always preserved as the untouched source.
