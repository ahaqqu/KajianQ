# KajianQ & DARS — Specification, Architecture & Plan

> KajianQ is an open-source Islamic classical-knowledge chatbot, and DARS is the domain-agnostic RAG engine beneath it. This document holds the architecture, the policies and the plan — `CONTEXT.md` holds the vocabulary and `adr/` holds the decisions.

---

## 1. Vision & Objectives

### 1.1 What this is

- **KajianQ** — an open-source (MIT) Islamic classical-knowledge chatbot for the **general Indonesian Muslim public** (not scholars), chatting in **Indonesian and English** over a corpus of **original Arabic sources**: Quran (Uthmani), hadith (with sanad and grades), and **pre-600 H kitab** (fiqh, aqidah, tasawuf, history). **DARS** (_Dynamic Automated RAG Solution_) — the generic, domain-agnostic RAG engine underneath: ingestion, Smart Router, retrieval, generation, evaluation. Engine code carries **no Islamic-domain logic**; KajianQ is a domain pack plus apps on top. Both live in **one monorepo** (ADR-0005): DARS as workspace packages under `packages/`, KajianQ under `apps/`. Reusability (public-service chat, internal-company knowledge) is a long-term goal enforced by package boundaries, not by building a platform prematurely.

### 1.2 Long-term mission

Improve the Islamic character (_akhlak_) of Indonesian Muslims — youth in particular — by making it easy to get answers grounded in classical Islamic sources. The v1 product serves the **general public**; the youth mission is served through accessibility (plain Indonesian, mobile-first PWA), not by narrowing the audience.

### 1.3 Success for v1

**Real public users at small scale**: the product is deployed, open-source, and used by real people, who receive trustworthy, strictly-sourced answers and can see the system improve through their feedback. That is why traceability, feedback, and evaluation are in v1 scope.

### 1.4 Differentiators (moat)

The corpus and the honesty of the answer: original Arabic plus pre-600 H kitab (Mudawwanah, Al-Umm, Ihya, Tabari, …) rather than Quran and Bukhari/Muslim alone; Indonesian-first chat with strict citations (`QS Surah:Ayah` · `HR Book No. (Grade)` · `Kitab, Author, Vol:Page:Bab`); madzhab filter and compare, Syafi'i-weighted with transparent gaps, once the corpus carries the metadata; Principle-aware retrieval, where general maxims are retrieved alongside the ruling; a **full user-facing Trace** of how each answer was built; and LLM-cleaned, LLM-translated, harness-validated data rather than a raw import.

### 1.5 Hard boundaries (never cross)

1. **Never issue personal fatwa.** The system presents what classical sources say, with citations. The canonical close accompanies every answer the Product rules decorate — a pure refusal is exempt — and is that answer's final paragraph, written once by the rule; a unit is deleted only when its normalised token sequence equals one of the rule's disclaimer templates, a closed set, so any unit carrying another token is preserved byte-for-byte beside the close (a paraphrase outside the set, or a byte-equal copy the draft embedded in a larger unit, stays where the draft put it) and no sentence is ever deleted for resembling a disclaimer (ADR-0057).
2. **Never answer beyond the retrieved context.** If retrieval is insufficient, say so explicitly.
3. **Never hide the machinery.** Every answer carries its full Trace (ADR-0007).
4. **Never silently machine-translate.** Kitab translations are labeled as machine translation, with the Arabic always shown (ADR-0006).
5. **Dhaif hadith is always flagged** with a warning.
6. **Never derive a new ruling.** The Generator surfaces classical reasoning (ta'lil, madzhab disagreement, applied Principles) _as cited_; it never synthesizes novel rulings, never resolves disagreements, never upgrades a grade. Insufficient evidence → refusal, not synthesis (ADR-0015).
7. **No corpus-wide inferred knowledge graphs.** Knowledge layers are bounded, curated, human-reviewed concept structures — the terminology graph, the isnad layer, the Principle Index, curated passage links — never a community-GraphRAG corpus extraction (ADR-0016).

## 2. Product Specification

### 2.1 Capabilities

- **Bilingual chat (ID/EN)** with automatic language detection, answers in the user's language, Arabic originals shown for every quoted passage, and **strict citations**: Quran `QS. Al-Baqarah:255`, Hadith `HR. Bukhari no. 573 (Sahih)`, Kitab `Al-Umm, Imam Syafi'i, Jilid 1, Hal. 102, Bab …`.
- **Madzhab policy (Syafi'i-weighted, transparent gaps).** The corpus ingests all madhhabs available; when the user does not specify, lead with the Syafi'i view (Indonesian majority) and note other madzhab views _if the corpus covers them_, otherwise state that they are not yet available. Users may set a preference or explicitly compare madhhabs once the corpus carries the metadata, and differences are presented neutrally, never attacking any school. **Hadith grades** (`mutawatir | sahih | hasan | dhaif`) are always displayed, and dhaif material carries a warning. **Principle-aware answers.** Why and analogy questions retrieve **Principles** (yusr, rahmah, masyaqqah, dharar, umum al-balwa, istihsan, sadd al-dhara'i, …) alongside specific rulings, so answers explain the _lens_, not just the rule.
- **Full Trace panel (user-facing).** Every answer expands to show the router intent, its sub-queries, the retrieved chunks with scores, the model identity, and the reviewer pre-gate's verdict when the trace recorded one: a readable "sources consulted" view first, technical detail one tap deeper. **Trace-anchored feedback.** Thumbs up/down per answer, plus flagging a specific Trace element — _wrong citation · irrelevant chunk · bad machine translation · questionable grade_ — with optional free text, anonymously and rate-limited. Feedback lands in the admin review queue, and accepted items become **Golden Set** cases. **Chat history and context awareness**, over durable sessions.

### 2.3 UI notes

React PWA from the template (TanStack Router/Query, Tailwind), mobile-first, with en/id externalized strings, an expandable Trace per message, and a feedback widget anchored to Trace elements. The visual language is a full-bleed sticky header over a 1152px column — a serif-italic wordmark and an 8-pointed-star logo tile at the left, the primary nav centered on large screens, the round controls at the right, and a left drawer below the large breakpoint — with editorial display headings on the static pages, a cream-and-dark-green light theme and a near-black-and-gold dark theme (the theme choice is persisted locally), and locally loaded fonts so the app works offline. The chat keeps a 768px reading measure, scrolls with the document so the page's own scrollbar serves the transcript, and pins the composer to the bottom edge. The chat is the home route: it rehydrates the transcript on load and on every return to the route, asks before it keeps anything in the browser (the answer is remembered and revocable, and until it is given the chat holds its session for the tab only), keeps the conversations it is allowed to keep in a local history list (the header's history drawer: the first question, the locale and the time, capped, reopenable, deletable locally) so a new conversation never strands the previous one, resolves citation chips from the structured citations frame (a passage sheet with the Arabic original RTL, the labeled translation, a grade badge, and the source), and renders the dhaif warning as a card and the ulama disclaimer as a per-answer footer. The public `/about`, `/collection`, `/faq`, `/privacy` and `/terms` routes render through the same shell and the shared footer; the Collection page's content is a typed data module mirroring the dataset notices and §4.1/§4.2, with a mandatory available/planned distinction, and the Art. 13 privacy register has its single home on `/privacy`.

## 3. Architecture

### 3.1 Monorepo (fork of `agentic-project-template`)

One GitHub repo, Bun workspaces. `apps/web` is the React PWA and `apps/api` the Hono API; `packages/` holds the engine and shared pieces — contracts (Valibot contracts for every API boundary), infra (logger, config store, object store, store and provider adapters), rag-core (the pipeline interfaces), rag-ingest (source parsers, cleaning and translation, chunking), eval (benchmark harness, Golden Set runner, judges), rate and hardening (the shared, project-agnostic adapters), and kajianq-domain (the domain pack: madzhab enums, principle seeds, prompts, citation formatters) — with `scripts/` holding the Bun ingestion and eval CLIs. Mechanical template-sync is retired: the fork had drifted far enough that every sync produced hand-resolved conflicts, so every file is project-owned. `packages/` remains the shared-engine merge path for a future DARS extraction; `apps/` and the root manifest are project composition, and ADRs live in root `adr/`. **Dropped from the template:** the local-first package (chat needs network anyway) and D1 with the Notes tracer feature. **Reused as-is:** anonymous-session auth, Valibot contracts, adapters, i18n, Vitest/fast-check/Playwright-BDD, and the CI gates (size-limit, agentic-limits). The template's Workers deploy pipeline was **not** kept: ADR-0044 replaced it with the VPS deployer — the "topology as code" idea stands, the Cloudflare implementation does not. **Stage communication** is in-process typed interfaces between packages (a modular monolith), never HTTP between pipeline stages; extract a service only when a second consumer actually appears.

**Composition and effect runtime (ADR-0027):** the engine packages and the API adopt Effect as the typed effect runtime — seam signatures return `Effect<A, E, R>`, the run context maps to tagged services, the pipeline runner owns each run's `Scope`, provider fallback retries through per-kind `Schedule`s, and SSE deltas are a `Stream` whose interruption reaches the provider fetch. The persistence seams are Effect-signatured too (ADR-0027 decision 7): the store and object-store seams return `Effect<A, StoreError, R>` with the closed `StoreError` taxonomy owned by the engine. The HTTP edge stays Hono + hono-openapi through an `Effect.runPromise` bridge, valibot remains the only schema vocabulary, and the frontend stays plain TypeScript — Effect is never exposed over the API contract. Permanently outside the Effect surface: the contracts, rate and hardening packages, migrations with their CLI, and the deploy path, each with a recorded reopen condition (ADR-0027 decision 7). **Fork guardrail amendment (ADR-0009):** the template's "never add paid services to the critical path" is amended — paid LLM and embedding APIs _are_ the critical path of a RAG product. Price is weighed in every model decision, cost is traced per query, and free tiers are used only where quality allows.

### 3.2 Runtime topology (ADR-0008)

The user reaches **Cloudflare DNS only** (the CDN proxy must stay off — ADR-0044 decision 4), which resolves to a **netcup VPS in Germany/EU** where nginx fronts `apps/api` as a plain Bun process and serves the static web build, with **Postgres + pgvector self-hosted on the same box** behind the store seam, a serving unit and a nightly session-reclaim unit under systemd, encrypted backups, and 14-day access logs; **Cloudflare R2** is the object store for raw corpus and snapshot dumps. External APIs — Gemini on free and paid terms, Qwen, DeepSeek, Ollama Cloud, and TypeSafe — sit behind the Provider and Decider seams (ADR-0009, ADR-0042, ADR-0044 decision 7, ADR-0059). **One instance, and never locked to one process (ADR-0050).** The box runs exactly one API process behind nginx, and that stays the posture until measured load says otherwise — but no code may assume it: every durable byte lives in Postgres behind the store seam or in the object-store seam, the request path carries no client affinity, and an answer is persisted before its SSE stream starts. Adding instances is a deployment change guarded by a closed list (a shared rate-limit counter, a pinned max connection count, an instance-addressable serving path, a single cron owner, a store-touching readiness probe); a second **host** is a new decision, because Postgres is loopback-only. The Cloudflare Workers + Neon path this replaces is **removed, not maintained in parallel** (ADR-0044 decision 2): there were no live users, so the migration was a single-shot cutover with no rollback runway — the git history is the rollback. Ingestion and the eval harness run as **Bun CLI scripts** (local/CI), never inside the serving runtime, each pointing at `DATABASE_URL`; that is a deliberate deviation from the template's D1, because the Smart Router needs SQL metadata filtering plus vector search that D1/Vectorize cannot express.

**Hosting decision (ADR-0043; serving path implemented under ADR-0044).** The serving path runs on a **netcup GmbH VPS (Germany/EU)** — EU/Germany residency for chat content and traces, which can reveal religious convictions (Art. 9 GDPR) — with Postgres self-hosted rather than on a managed free plan. Because KajianQ serves the public, the household exception does not apply and a DPA under Art. 28(3) is mandatory with netcup GmbH as processor, concluded in netcup's Customer Control Panel (Master Data → Order Processing). The sub-processor register and the retention values live in `docs/GDPR-ARTICLE-30-RECORD.md`, which is their source of truth: ADR-0043 decision 3 records the register rule the notice enforces and decision 4 the retention decisions. The register rule is ADR-0009's, made enforceable: personal data never routes through a vendor's free tier, enforced per call by `personalDataAllowed` filtering for any call that declares personal data, and every serving call site declares it. The reviewer runs same-vendor deliberately — the deterministic validator and the reviewer gate carry the trust invariant, not vendor separation (ADR-0044 decision 7). **Provisioning and deploys (ADR-0044).** The deployer is its own concern, outside `apps/`: its path is triggered by a reusable workflow that Staging calls, so one implementation exists. Provisioning stays under `provision/vps/` and CI gates stay in the workflows; the deploy builds self-contained bundles, so the box needs no `node_modules`, and local dev and e2e boot the same Bun entry the box runs. The deploy identity is a dedicated account holding exactly two granted commands, shipped as code and pinned in agreement by a test that also fails on an unused grant or a wildcard. A deploy refuses a box whose provisioned files do not match the checkout, so a merged provisioning change cannot stay inert behind a green deploy. VPS privacy hardening is provisioned as code: a minimal access-log format (client IP and request line, no user-agent or referrer), 14-day logrotate for the proxy and Postgres, a 14-day journald cap, the loopback-only Postgres posture, an unprivileged service unit, the nightly reclamation, and daily encrypted backups (client-side encryption, 30-day rolling, key outside the repo) whose restore refuses a target naming the live database and re-applies erasure — proven by a restore drill in CI. On-host steps: `docs/VPS-SETUP.md`; the operator's manual: `docs/VPS-OPERATIONS.md`; corpus ingestion: `docs/CORPUS-INGEST.md`.

### 3.3 The DARS pipeline (Smart Router)

1. **Intent & Principle detection** (cheap tier) → one JSON payload: `intent (factual|ruling|analogy|comparison|history|aqidah), category, madzhab, grade, textLayer, needsPrinciple, principleTags, confidence, reasoning, subQueries`. Stages 1 and 2 ride **one** call — a second call would double router latency and cost for information one JSON object already carries (ADR-0051). The domain reads the reply into closed vocabularies and drops a value outside them rather than force-matching it; the caller's explicit filters always win, and the classification lands on the answer's Trace. A reply the vocabularies cannot read — unparseable JSON, or an intent outside the list — takes the recorded **router fallback**: one factual sub-query made of the verbatim question, marked as a fallback, with the failed call's cost still on the trace, so the Trace never presents an invented classification as the model's reading.
2. **Query decomposition** (cheap tier) → 2–4 **Sub-queries**: always one factual when the question carries a letter or a digit; +principle if `needsPrinciple`; +Quranic dalil if the category is fikih; +sanad verification if hadith. The route is **gated on the caller's question before any model call**: a question carrying no letter and no digit is not a query — no classification is asked for, the fan-out is empty, nothing is embedded or searched, and the deterministic fallback is marked content-free (ADR-0051). The model phrases its own sub-queries and the domain **repairs** the set deterministically: the caller's verbatim question is always a sub-query of its own, carrying the factual rule's role and the rule's origin rather than the model's, beside the model's paraphrases — a paraphrase is an additive angle and never the only factual query (ADR-0061) — and every rule that fired has a sub-query carrying its role (a missing one is added from a template, and a rule whose own text is already present under no role has its role stamped onto that entry, unless the model labelled that entry with a different declared role). The total is bounded to 2–4 by spending the budget on one sub-query per distinct role before the model's extra angles — the caller's entry first, so the cap spends a model paraphrase rather than the caller's words — and is exactly **1** when the only retrieval text is the question itself. No text carrying no letter or digit is ever embedded, whatever its origin, and no single-query ablation switch exists (ADR-0051).
3. **Source routing** (rules + cheap tier) → source selection and SQL metadata filters (`sourceType`, `grade`), applied as an exact value match on the stored metadata field rather than a text search — except that `grade: sahih` also selects a row whose collection is one of the sahihayn and whose grade is absent, because that row is read as sahih (ADR-0060); the derived case is supplied to the store as data, so the engine names neither a grade nor a collection and every other dimension and value keeps exact equality. **Only dimensions the corpus carries are filters:** the router still classifies Madzhab, TextLayer and Principle and records those classifications on the answer's Trace, but they are not filter dimensions while no stored row carries their keys. The **rules** decide and the model only hints (ADR-0052): the sources are the **union** of what the reply's subject area implies (`quran` and `tafsir` → the Quran, its commentary, and the Sunnah that carries the practice asked about — one shared scriptural breadth, so which of the two the reading settles cannot change what is searched; `hadith` → Hadith; `fikih` → Quran, Sunnah, and Kitab, the authority order spent as breadth) and what **every sub-query's own role** implies (`dalil` and `sanad` → Hadith; `principle` → the Principle Index — schema-only and unseeded, so the selection returns no Principle rows until it is seeded; `factual` → nothing of its own), derived from the sub-queries retrieval actually fans out over, so no part the router decomposed is unsearchable. The category is a sample, so no row decides whether the Sunnah is in reach: every non-empty selection names it. An `analogy` intent or a needed Principle adds the Principle Index; an area no rule covers selects **none**, because an unfiltered search already covers every part — a part's role or the lens rule may widen a selection but never narrow it back, and the caller's own filters always win. Both maps are exhaustive over the vocabularies that own them, so a category or a role added without a source decision is a typecheck failure, and a dimension the store cannot express — or the corpus cannot satisfy — **fails the run** rather than being dropped. A zero-hit search **probes** one dimension at a time, and only an adopted drop is kept, so the filter record any search ran with is the intended record minus every adopted drop; a sweep that adopts nothing ends in **one** record-level retry with an empty filter record, so a wrong hint cannot turn into a refusal for a question the corpus can serve. The decision is typed trace content: the routing event carries the selected sources and the exact filter record of the decision, while the store receives that same decision with each value's derived case attached as data — the event records the decision, never the widened record the search binds.
4. **Retrieval** — dense only: every sub-query is embedded and searched against both embedding tracks, and the per-track, per-sub-query lists are fused with **RRF (k=60)** plus hierarchy bonuses (Quran +0.3, Sahih +0.25, Hasan +0.15, Kitab +0.1, Principle +0.2 on analogy). **The raw searches are trace content:** every sub-query × track search records its track, its full hit count, and the chunk ids it returned with their dense ranks — ids and ranks only, the search's whole returned list within its own bound — beside the fused `retrieval` event, so a row missing from the fused set is distinguishable from one the search never returned. No lexical or sparse arm runs: retrieval issues no full-text query and reads no text index. All five bonuses apply; four are properties of the chunk and the fifth is a property of the **question** — a Principle chunk takes its bonus only when the route read `intent: "analogy"` — while a weak grade takes nothing: dhaif is flagged, never boosted (ADR-0052). **The grade is read, not only stored (ADR-0060):** a hadith with no stored grade whose collection is one of the two sahihayn is read as sahih for the Sahih bonus and for a `grade: sahih` filter, because the collection is the evidence — so the authority order the prompt states is not inverted for the collections it names. Nothing is written, so a citation label renders exactly as ingested; a stored grade always wins, and a row with no grade outside the two collections stays ungraded and takes no bonus. **Surah scoped expansion (ADR-0045):** a deterministic, bounded read of a surah parent's children is added _alongside_ the fused hits. Two triggers issue it: the verbatim question's own reference — an explicit address, a name behind a marker word, or a description the domain pack's curated table carries — because a short formulaic verse is an unreachable semantic neighbour of a whole-surah meta-question; and a retrieved verse whose surah fits the budget, read from that surah's opening, so a short surah retrieval entered is completed rather than left partly outside the context. Detection of the reference runs on the verbatim question, never on a sub-query; the window begins at the reference (the parent's opening, or the named verse's own position), the two triggers share one cap (default 12), and an explicit address's verse is kept only when the surah has it, so no trace names a phantom verse. A description the table does not carry gets no read and falls back to fused retrieval, a recorded residual rather than a semantic match. **Retrieved-verse neighbourhood expansion (ADR-0049):** a retrieved verse anchors a bounded read of the verses immediately around it, so a citation range the generator writes is in context. It is bounded twice — radius and cap, read at the composition root; `0` on either disables the path, and a negative value is a typed config failure — and a query whose context holds no verse issues no store read; every added chunk carries its own origin on the trace and resolves to the read that produced it. **Citation-label lookup (ADR-0058):** a citation label the verbatim question names — a hadith's collection and number, an ayah's surah and ayah — is read deterministically from the corpus's stored identity and added _alongside_ the fused hits, because an exact number is an identifier rather than a topic; a label no stored row carries adds nothing, so the canonical refusal stays the answer. The read is capped (zero disables it), the chunks it adds carry their own origin, and the typed event records the labels named, the misses, and the citation-shaped forms the question wrote that the arm declined to resolve, with why. The read is identity-addressed, so a label the caller named outranks the route's inferred filters — the same footing as the scoped and neighbour reads — while the grade rule still flags a weak chunk it brings in. **Range grounding (ADR-0049):** a citation naming a range grounds only when **every** address it names is present, a range the surah cannot hold refuses, and a range of one address is decided as a declared list. **Decision-model rerank (ADR-0042):** the fused candidates are reordered by one batched decision call before context assembly, ordered by the answer's probability distribution; the question's wording is the domain pack's and the engine names neither a source type nor a citation. The ranking is an ordering only — the unlabelled fused block is reordered, none added or dropped — and a vendor failure, an unusable answer, or an answer contradicting its own distribution leaves the fused order standing (fail-open), with the refusing rule recorded. A labelled chunk keeps the position retrieval gave it, so the deterministic arms' chunks and the caller-priority guarantee's rows are not offered for reordering, and when fewer than two unlabelled candidates remain no call is made and the block keeps the retriever's order. Both the fused score and the model's probability ride the typed rerank event, and the call's spend rides its sibling call event. It is active wherever the decision vendor's key is bound, the same key and posture as the reviewer pre-gate. **The caller's own query is a priority angle (ADR-0062):** fusion weighs every list alike, so the paraphrase lists can outscore the caller's single list; the leading dense hits of the sub-query carrying the caller's verbatim question — the decomposition repair's factual entry, or the router's deterministic fallback entry when no model reading routed the query — are therefore guaranteed into the context whatever their fusion rank, labelled with their own provenance so the rerank keeps their position, and led in their presentation slot — in the order that search ranked them, ahead of every unlabelled row in it — while the rest of the slot keeps the rerank's order. The guarantee is bounded by a configured cap defaulting to the contract's ceiling, selects from the list the caller's own search already returned — no embed, store read or vendor call — and records a typed `caller_priority` event naming the bound, the ids, and the rows the returned set already held.
5. **Context assembly** — presentation order: Principles → Quran → Tafsir (immediately after the Quran whose commentary it is) → Hadith → Kitab → everything the order does not name, sorted below every named source rather than interleaved by score into a slot it was never given; parent summaries are generated and stored at ingestion but are not part of the assembled context, so a retrieved child carries no chapter context. Within a slot the caller-priority guarantee's rows lead in the order retrieval ranked them; the reranked score decides the unlabelled block when a rerank applied, else the fused score. This order is deliberately distinct from the prompt's authority order — the distinction is the acceptance criterion, because an assembler that sorted the evidence by _authority_ would still produce a coherent, well-cited answer, just one whose lens arrives after the ruling it was meant to frame, and no gate reads the order.
6. **Generation** (quality tier) — strict grounding system prompt, streaming (ADR-0052). The prompt answers only from the retrieved context and forbids adding tafsir, takwil, scholarly opinion, or meaning glosses the context does not carry — including ones the model knows to be correct. It states the authority order — Quran → Hadith (mutawatir > sahih > hasan; dhaif flagged) → Tafsir → Kitab — in order, in both languages, scoped to what the context already carries, so it can never license reaching past the grounding rule above it, and it names the Principle lens as the reading frame rather than as evidence standing on its own. When a question demands one specific fact the context does not state — a year, date, number, or name — the generator emits the canonical refusal rather than an explanation that the fact is unknown, the directed second reading included: naming a passage does not license a statement of what it omits. **Evidence present is answered** (ADR-0015): a question whose asked fact the context states is answered even where the context describes that thing under another name, and a request to compare positions or name the most excellent among them is answered from what the context states about them — the absence of an explicit ranking is not an absent fact, and no ranking is invented. **A question carrying no letter and no digit is not generated for at all**: the run is declined with the product's canned, localized decline, and the generator and the reviewer are never entered, so the class makes no provider call and its trace carries no cost (ADR-0053). **A refusal is read twice before it ships** (ADR-0055): only a pure refusal is reread, on the same assembled evidence — or, when the verbatim question names a citation address the assembled context carries, with that passage named and the reading pointed at it, the canonical refusal staying available for what the context does not state — and the refusal is delivered only when the second reading is a pure refusal too, so a draft that answers — a hybrid partial answer carrying the sentence included — is never reread, an empty context is not reread, a question naming no address, or naming one the assembled context does not carry, keeps the plain second reading, a reread that cannot be read leaves the first refusal standing, and every reading's cost, a failed attempt's estimate included, stays on the trace.
7. **Post-processing** — citation validator (deterministic), grade flags, disclaimer, ID/EN formatting, and a sampled faithfulness review enqueued. A fabricated citation becomes the plain refusal, and the product's deterministic rules are written by code, never hoped from the prompt, and never applied to a pure refusal: the dhaif warning and the machine-translation label are appended when the draft lacks the product's copy. The canonical close accompanies every answer the Product rules decorate — a pure refusal is exempt — and is that answer's final paragraph, written once by the rule; a unit is deleted only when its normalised token sequence equals one of the rule's disclaimer templates, a closed set, so any unit carrying another token is preserved byte-for-byte beside the close (a paraphrase outside the set, or a byte-equal copy the draft embedded in a larger unit, stays where the draft put it) and no sentence is ever deleted for resembling a disclaimer (ADR-0057). Deltas are emitted only after the reviewer validated the **complete** answer, so an ungrounded citation is never a prefix already delivered; the reviewer is non-optional on the chat path (a role with no keyed candidate is a typed config failure and the route answers 503), and it is shown exactly the generator's evidence, so the gate cannot fail an answer for quoting what the prompt provided. The persisted Trace's event vocabulary is closed and typed (ADR-0007); the client renders server-derived frames — the citations frame (ADR-0040) and the two-layer trace frame — and never reconstructs pipeline machinery from raw trace events.

### 3.4 Providers & default model mix (ADR-0009)

Generation and embedding calls ride a **Provider interface**; decision-model calls (ADR-0042) ride the **Decider seam** — typed choice and score questions over a state, not chat, so never in a Provider fallback chain. Every stage's model is config-swappable, and the assignments with their prices live in the model config (`packages/infra/src/providers/models.json`). **Vendor allowlist: Gemini (a free-tier row and a paid-terms row), DeepSeek, Ollama Cloud, Qwen, and TypeSafe AI (decision-model only, bench-gated, ADR-0042).** The tie-break is capability first: near-equal capability → prefer Qwen.

The chat serving chains are selected by a **named provider profile** (ADR-0059): the `default` profile is the mix below, and the `ollama` profile re-heads the generator, reviewer, reviewer-live and cheap chains on Ollama Cloud while the embedder, ingestion translation and decision vendor are untouched. A profile is one deployment value; an unknown name is a wiring-time configuration failure, and the default profile stays the rollback. Ollama Cloud carries personal data on its zero-retention/no-training terms with **no signed DPA on file** — the owner-accepted basis recorded in the register and ADR-0059.

| Role                                              | Default                                    | Challenger                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Embedding                                         | Gemini embedding on the **paid-terms** row | Qwen3-Embedding                                                 |
| Cheap tier (router stages 1–3, tagging, cleaning) | DeepSeek V4-Flash (serving head)           | Gemini Flash free-tier tail, skipped for personal-data calls    |
| Generator                                         | DeepSeek V4-Flash (cost head)              | Qwen3.7-Max (quality head)                                      |

**The reviewer is the escalation tier, and the decision-model pre-gate fronts it (ADR-0042).** The adoption landed inside the existing Reviewer stage — no new pipeline stage, no runner change. Order per answer: deterministic citation validator (free) → **one batched decision call** screening every citation against the claim it supports → the LLM reviewer **only on escalation**. An answer whose citations all clear is reviewed-clean and skips the paid reviewer call; any citation below threshold, any missing or malformed answer, any decision-vendor failure, and any draft with no citation to judge escalate to the full LLM reviewer exactly as before. The pre-gate can only _remove_ spend on an answer it affirmatively cleared, so reviewer safety never depends on it (fail-open). It is always active wherever the decision vendor's key is bound — no enable/disable flag exists — and every pre-gate call is traced: its spend on the review stage's call event, and its verdict on the typed decision event (outcome, threshold, per-citation scores, escalation reason). The same keyed role also carries the retrieval rerank (§3.3 stage 4): one decider, two adoptions, and unbinding the key stops both. The Golden Set run is the standing duty: no refusal inflation and no citation-validity regression. **The embedding default is gate-decided (ADR-0036), and the serving row is the paid-terms one.** Per ADR-0013, retrieval is Arabic-canonical: the Arabic text and its embedding are the primary evidence index, and the Indonesian track is a built-from-the-start fallback and fusion track. The benchmark gate compared the candidate models on cross-lingual and Arabic recall over the real corpus plus the terminology-expansion micro-task, and the dual-index schema keeps the posture switchable without re-embedding. A free tier's traffic may be used to train the vendor's models — acceptable for public religious content and anonymous queries, but **never for a serving call**: the retriever embeds the user's sub-queries, which are personal data, so the embedder chain head is the same model on paid terms (a separate vendor row, because the register rule keys on terms, not models), and the already-ingested corpus vectors stay valid with no re-embed. The free-tier row survives for non-personal calls (ingestion, tagging). The harness (`packages/eval`) holds versioned test sets — Arabic comprehension, Indonesian generation, citation discipline, translation fidelity — scoring every candidate; results are stored and visible in admin, and re-run on model releases.

### 3.5 Data layer

- **Corpus.** Parent documents (book or chapter, carrying an LLM summary) and child passages, with **dual embeddings** per passage per ADR-0013: Arabic canonical plus Indonesian fallback and fusion, both built from the start. The **Principle Index**, **curated passage links**, and the **terminology concept graph** (ADR-0014) are planned knowledge layers: their schemas exist, but they hold no data and have no ingestion path or consumer, so none is a working layer. The graph is a separate term-level layer — language-neutral concept nodes, per-language lemmas with their own embeddings, typed relations, and lemma evidence aligned to ayat — distinct from passage-level links. Quran ingestion also writes one row per **aligned (Arabic, Indonesian) ayah pair**, the seed corpus that graph is designed to consume — the pairs alone do not build it.
- **Sessions and chat.** Anonymous sessions (ADR-0017) and their chat sessions and messages. A session is a 30-day Bearer identity; `DELETE /v1/auth/me` erases the user and the whole subtree (sessions, chat rows, traces, feedback) in one transaction whose success means the rows are gone, because a surviving row aborts it, and a nightly job reclaims expired sessions together with the anonymous users left without one.
- **Answer traces.** The persisted `Trace` contract verbatim (ADR-0007): router intent and classification, sub-queries with their roles and origins, retrieved chunk refs with their fused scores, model identities, tokens, cost and latency, and each refusal, review, decision and product-rule event. The user-facing Trace panel and the admin browser read server-derived frames built from these traces, never raw events shipped to the client.
- **Feedback, ledger, config.** A thumb or an anchored flag against a persisted, owned trace — one verdict per (user, answer, element), anchored to an answer, chunk, citation, translation, or grade, with optional free text, cascade-deleted with the user; the Golden Set questions, eval runs, and per-question results that form the §3.7 ledger; and a registry mirroring the model assignment files for admin display, the files remaining the source of truth: the mirror is refreshed from the effective configuration at startup within a bounded wait — a refresh that cannot complete in that window is logged and skipped rather than delaying the listener — and an admin detail read whose mirror disagrees with that configuration is refused rather than decorating a trace with a retired model or price. Ingestion is **idempotent**: re-runnable upserts key on natural identifiers, and raw source text is **immutable** — cleaning and translation write new fields, never overwrite the original. Matn and Sharh are never mixed in one chunk, and disputed attributions are quarantined or labeled, never force-merged.

### 3.7 Integration testing (real)

- **Golden Set**: roughly 50–100 ID/EN questions with expected source types, required citations, and known traps (dhaif hadith, cross-madzhab differences, untranslated kitab, refusal cases). The set is curated and owner-signed, and a `requiredCitations` label must be reachable in the corpus the gate runs against: re-curating one because that corpus does not carry it is a **recorded curation change**, never a silent re-label. The question text is the gate's input, so a re-word is a **recorded curation change** like a re-curation. A question id is the result ledger's key: a retired question leaves the set and its id is never reused, its replacement taking a new id, and a retirement is a recorded curation change like a re-curation. A parallel chain of a required hadith is the same Matn identity, and so is another narration that states its ruling or stated merit (ADR-0054): the fixture keeps one required label per requirement, and the curated, owner-signed matn-identity map records the narrations the identity covers and **why** they are related — both the `same-report` relation of one report's chains and the `same-ruling` relation of another narration gate citation validity, because the map, not a single address, is the gate's unit of identity; adding a chain or a ruling narration is a recorded curation change like re-curating a label. A curated **verse pair** records the same way: two addresses the owner rules one piece of evidence, each entry carrying the pair's rationale, never a rule that merely shares a name. A single-requirement row may carry an opt-in, owner-curated **alternative set** — different reports the owner rules equally acceptable answers to that question — and only that opt-in widens the requirement: a member is accepted only with verified verbatim evidence naming that member's collection, address and grade, and it is matched as its own normalized label, never as an entry point into its identity family; every other row keeps the all-required form. A row whose required address anchors a signed `same-ruling` relation may repeat a member in its opt-in set so the exact-address measure accepts it too; every candidate the curator read and ruled out is recorded in the row's structured exclusion field, with the reason in a one-line note. Citation validity may accept a parallel chain or a curated alternative as the required narration, but retrieval address recall still requires the fixture's own address — or an alternative the row opted in to — to have been retrieved. A fabricated-attribution trap targets material the store's collections do not carry, so the answer must refuse rather than stretch to a present-but-weak hadith — a curation-gate question, recorded rather than hidden.
- **Metrics**: two retrieval dimensions — **source-type recall** (the expected source types the retrieved chunks carry) and **citation-address recall** (the specific addresses the fixture's required citations name) — both scored from the served pipeline's persisted answer trace, never a re-run, plus citation validity (deterministic) and faithfulness (LLM judge, wired as the pipeline reviewer), and two **report-only** measures: **quote fidelity** — whether the words an answer puts in quotation marks appear in the text of the chunks it cites, absent when there is nothing to measure and read by no gate, so the measurement never moves a question's pass or fail — and **identity-satisfied address recall**, the gate's dimensions re-scored so a signed identity also satisfies the requirement (the exact address still counts), reported with the count of rows it would pass. Both are read by no gate, so a run's pass or fail never moves on either. Source-type recall answers whether retrieval reached the right **kind** of source; address recall answers whether it reached the required **address**, joining the trace's chunk references to their citation metadata in the citation gate's comparison form. Every entry in a question's required-citation list is required; a row may opt in to a curated alternative set that widens exactly one requirement, and without that opt-in there is no any-of form. A question with none is trivially address-complete. Address recall is deliberately stricter than citation validity: a parallel narration or another narration of the required Matn's ruling satisfies citation validity, but only the required address — or a curated alternative the row opted in to — satisfies address recall. The identity-satisfied measure is the reported counterfactual that also lets a signed identity satisfy the address leg: it counts a signed relation of either kind, while the gate's exact-address number stays exact and comparable with past runs. The full suite passes a question only when both recall dimensions, citation validity and behaviour are all satisfied, so a run whose fused set reaches the right source types while missing the required addresses reports the gap instead of a full score; the deployed smoke **reports** address recall and prints the misses its own sample produced — that run's measurement, not a hand-maintained debt list — but gates only on source-type recall, citation validity and behaviour, so a small sample cannot redden a deploy on a miss that is not a regression. Citation validity reads its evidence in a fixed order — the citations frame when the transport carries one, else the trace's review-event grounded labels, else the answer text through the citation gate's own grammar — so a fabricated citation cannot pass. Citation validity judges **Matn identity** (ADR-0054): a hadith label resolves through the curated, owner-signed matn-identity map to its canonical narration, and the required citation is present when the evidence carries any label of that identity under either signed relation; an unmapped, fabricated, or unrelated label is its own identity and cannot match another. A hybrid refusal is scored on the citations its text actually carries, while the refusal dimension reads the trace's refusal event, so an `answer` question a hybrid draft answered is still scored as an over-refusal. **Trap acceptance (ADR-0046):** a `refuse` question is accepted on a **refusal** or a **grounded answer** carrying at least one citation the existing evidence verifies; a non-refusal with no verified citation still fails, and an `answer` question that is refused still fails (over-refusal is a defect). The prohibition on asserting a demanded-but-absent date is prompt-enforced only — an accepted residual. **Range citations are checked per address (ADR-0049):** a dash-joined range names every address in it, bounded by the surah's own ayah count, so it grounds exactly when all of them were retrieved and refuses when any was not — a range the grammar cannot enumerate refuses in every spelling. **Cost and exit policy**: one hard budget accumulator caps harness plus pipeline spend, with a fail-closed abort, and the exit policy is shared by the full suite and the smoke — non-zero on any failed, skipped, or **unmeasured** question, because an unmeasured question is not a pass; a skip's cause is persisted per question and printed in the run summary.
- **Cadence**: the deployed gate is the cost-capped smoke subset on every code-bearing staging deploy, not in the PR gate set, and the full suite is operator-invoked at the documented release gate; the recorded run's evidence is replayed offline as a CI pin, which gates on both recall dimensions. No nightly run, and none is planned — a nightly would re-measure, on a schedule, the commit the merge gate already measured, at real vendor prices. Results live in admin; the template's unit, property, and BDD gates are unchanged. The smoke subset is stratified so a refusal case, the label classes the product requires (the dhaif trap and the cross-madzhab answer), and an Indonesian answer case are always present before the budget fills by stable order; a required class no question carries, or that the budget cannot fit, fails the gate rather than shrinking the subset. A case the corpus carries but the deployed commit's pipeline cannot decide consistently — a required citation its retrieval cannot ground, a classification its router samples — is held out of the sample and stays in the full set and QA, and the run's plan reports every class and source type the sample no longer exercises. **Where the smoke runs**: in the `Staging` workflow's post-deploy checks job, after every staging deploy — a push or merge outside the docs-and-tests exclusion, and an operator dispatch. That deployment is the one deployment, and `staging` is its environment label (no production is provisioned, ADR-0044 decision 2). Docs-only or tests-only pushes cannot change the deployed site, so they skip the push trigger, since a deploy plus its paid smoke would be pure spend with no new artifact to verify; a lint script fails when an excluded path reaches the deploy entries' static import closure. The deploy job guards the secrets before deploying, and the checks job then mints its own anonymous session and runs the smoke against the deployed site with a budget cap always set, so a deploy that breaks grounded answering or the refusal gate cannot go green, and a failed deploy skips the checks instead of scoring a stale environment. **The smoke waits for readiness before it samples**: it polls the health route within a stated budget, and a `/v1/chat` round-trip that ends without a response — a failed connection or a 502/503/504 edge rejection — is the same readiness problem on any question, retried with backoff out of one per-run retry budget and then reported as readiness rather than as a skip. A request that got its response headers and then broke mid-stream is answered-then-failed and is never retried. A skip therefore means a question could not be measured — a real failure after the target answered, or a readiness failure whose retries were spent — and a run that never got an answer records its unasked questions as skips while the verdict is the readiness failure, not a mid-run outage. A target that never becomes ready reds the run without asking anything. A smoke failure does not skip the checks that follow it: the DAST scan and the API fuzz run regardless, and the job still ends red. The gate is deploy-then-smoke; there is no PR-time preview.
- **Staging corpus**: the smoke scores recall from persisted traces, so staging must hold a real corpus, loaded once per environment by an operator running the ingest CLIs against `DATABASE_URL` and verifying the landed source-type counts. The Staging workflow carries **no** ingest job (ADR-0037): a money-spending job must never sit in the same workflow graph as the evidence job, because a failed ingest would then skip the smoke — spending money and producing no evidence. Ingest is idempotent in persistence but **not** in cost: a pass re-embeds every row it reads, so resumption is by measured store state, never by re-dispatching. **The gate scores behaviour, never corpus coverage**, so a PR citing the smoke's evidence must say plainly that it is a behavioural gate.

### 4.4 Ingestion pipelines

## 5. Cost Model

Cost is dominated by the generator and the reviewer, not by retrieval: a query embedding is negligible and the deterministic stages are free. The retrieval rerank adds one input-priced decision call per retrieval that offers at least two fused candidates to rank, on the request's critical path: assembly waits for it, so every such query pays its latency as well as its spend. A retrieval whose fused block is smaller makes no call and pays neither. The rerank reorders the candidates retrieval already produced, so no candidate enters or leaves the assembled set. A route the gate declined is the one free query — its answer is canned copy, never a generation (ADR-0053). A refusal pays the generator twice, because the refusal must be read twice before it ships (ADR-0055), the directed second reading replacing the plain one rather than adding to it; an empty context has nothing to reread, so that refusal pays once; a draft the reread rescues from a refusal also pays twice — the refused reading plus the shipped one — and a first-reading answer pays once. The values — per-model prices, and the config row for every role — live in the model config (`packages/infra/src/providers/models.json`), never here.

- **What dominates.** The assembled context is the cost driver of a clean answer: retrieval searches each track with a fixed per-query limit, fuses every hit, appends the chunks the deterministic arms added, and the assembler renders the whole set — there is no post-fusion cut — so the context size is what those limits and the arms' own caps make it. Prompt caching for the stable system prompt and Principle blocks, and context trimming, are the levers. The deterministic retrieval paths (§3.3 stage 4) are bounded by their own caps, and a query's combined ceiling is the sum of them.

- **The escalation tier is pre-gated.** The reviewer is the expensive second line, so the decision-model pre-gate screens every citation first: a clean answer pays only the pre-gate, and an escalated answer pays both. The escalation rate is what the Golden Set run and the trace's decision events measure (ADR-0042).
- **One-time ingestion** is cheap relative to dirty data in production — translation plus cleaning, tagging, and embeddings per kitab.
- **Fixed versus variable after the hosting move (ADR-0043/ADR-0044).** The per-query lines do not depend on where the API runs. What changed is the fixed line: two free tiers became the netcup VPS contract, with Cloudflare kept for DNS and at-rest R2 archives, and the encrypted backup target as its own line — it holds a full-database dump, so it must not be a free tier (the register rule, ADR-0009/ADR-0043). EU/Germany residency is bought with a flat monthly cost, not a per-query one; the free-plan ceiling is gone as a cost and scale constraint, replaced by disk sizing on the VPS.

## 9. v2 Plan — Sanadset: Isnad data & per-chain grades (targeted for v2.0)

### 9.1 Goal

Close v1's integrity gap. v1 stores one flattened grade per matn, but hadith science grades **chains, not texts**: the same matn can be sahih via one isnad and dhaif via another, and weak chains can strengthen each other (hasan li-ghayrihi). A trust-first product cannot stay confidently wrong in this class of answers. v2 makes grades per-chain with grader attribution and shows transmission evidence (ADR-0012).

### 9.2 Scope

- **In:** Sanadset ingestion (hadith tagged sanad/matn); narrator and chain tables; reconciliation against the v1 hadith corpus; per-chain grades in citations, answers, UI, and Trace; narrator-reliability answers; a Golden Set v2 gate. **Out:** full GraphRAG or any graph DB (ADR-0012 — recursive SQL suffices); narrator biographies beyond the Sanadset fields; non-hadith chains (for example tafsir isnads) — later.

### 9.3 Data layer (additive migration)

Two additive tables — narrators and hadith chains — with v1 rows, embeddings, and indexes untouched: no re-ingestion and no re-indexing.

### 9.4 Ingestion

Acquire (Kaggle; **verify licence terms first**) → parse sanad/matn tags → narrator entity resolution (deterministic Arabic name normalization, LLM assist on unresolved cases, sampled cross-vendor review, variants recorded) → matn reconciliation (exact → normalized → fuzzy with LLM adjudication; low-confidence matches quarantined, never force-merged) → attach chains; the ingestion report carries match rate, resolution rate, quarantine count, and cost.

### 9.5 Retrieval, generation, UI

Retrieval filters gain chain grade and narrator; citations name the chain and its grader with multiple chains presented neutrally and dhaif flagged as in v1; the prompt never upgrades a grade beyond retrieved evidence and notes corroboration when chains support it; the Trace shows the chains consulted.

### 9.6 Eval & gate

Golden Set v2 traps (ID/EN): conflicting grades per matn, corroboration cases, narrator reliability, and takhrij. The v1 suite must stay green, and a full gate runs before v2.0.

### 9.7 Sequence

The v2 work is sequenced schema → ingestion → per-chain grades in retrieval, UI and Trace → the Golden Set v2 and v2.0 gate, indicatively 4–6 weeks, starting after v1.0 (§7).

### 9.8 Risks

Narrator name-resolution errors, wrong matn matches, and Kaggle licensing are handled by the verification discipline in §9.4; scope creep toward GraphRAG is an explicit out of scope (§9.2, ADR-0012).
